‹ FolderVault

Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

FolderVault is a macOS app that backs up folders on your Mac to your own Google Drive. It runs only on your Mac. It has no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and its developer never receives your data.

Google data FolderVault accesses

  • Your Google Drive files (the drive permission): to create backup folders, upload and update backed-up files, move deleted files to Drive's trash, and let you browse, upload, download, move, rename and trash files yourself.
  • Your account name, email address and storage usage: shown in the app so you know which account is connected and how much space is left.

How it's used

FolderVault uses this data only to provide the features you see in the app. It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads your data.

FolderVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What's stored, and where

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.
  • Your backup settings, backup history and a list of the files each backup has uploaded are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/FolderVault on your Mac.
  • Your files are sent only to your own Google Drive, over an encrypted connection.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in FolderVault's settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete FolderVault's local data by removing the folder above.
  • Your backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there.
