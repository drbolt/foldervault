FolderVault

A personal macOS app that backs up folders you choose to your own Google Drive, on a schedule.

What it does

  • Keeps a copy of each folder you pick in your Google Drive. New and changed files are uploaded; files you delete on your Mac are moved to Drive's trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.
  • Runs backups by hand or on a schedule (every few hours, daily or weekly).
  • Shows your Mac and your Drive side by side so you can browse, upload, download, move, rename and trash files.

Who it's for

FolderVault is a private, single-user app. It isn't distributed or sold, and it has no servers of its own: it runs entirely on your Mac and talks only to Google.
